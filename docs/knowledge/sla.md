# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This SLA applies to all paid AgentFlow AI subscription plans.

## Uptime commitment

- Enterprise plan: 99.9% monthly uptime.
- Professional plan: 99.5% monthly uptime.
- Starter plan: 99.0% monthly uptime.

Uptime is measured on the API and dashboard, excluding scheduled maintenance
windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.

## Support response times

| Priority | Enterprise | Professional | Starter |
|----------|------------|--------------|---------|
| Critical | 1 hour     | 4 hours      | 8 hours |
| High     | 4 hours    | 8 hours      | 24 hours |
| Medium   | 8 hours    | 24 hours     | 48 hours |
| Low      | 24 hours   | 48 hours     | 72 hours |

Response time is the time to first substantive reply from support, measured
within business hours for Starter and Professional, and around the clock for
Enterprise.

## Service credits

When monthly uptime falls below the committed level, customers may request a
service credit within 30 days of the incident:

- Uptime between 99.0% and the committed level: 10% of the monthly fee.
- Uptime between 95.0% and 99.0%: 25% of the monthly fee.
- Uptime below 95.0%: 50% of the monthly fee.

Credits are applied to the next invoice. Credits are the sole remedy for SLA
breaches; cash refunds are not issued for downtime.

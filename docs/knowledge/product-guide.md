# AgentFlow AI Product Guide

AgentFlow AI is a customer operations platform for B2B SaaS teams. It routes
customer tickets through automated multi-agent workflows with human approval
for risky actions.

## Plans

- **Starter** — up to 5 seats, community support, 99.0% uptime SLA.
- **Professional** — up to 50 seats, business-hours support, 99.5% uptime SLA,
  workflow analytics.
- **Enterprise** — unlimited seats, 24/7 support, 99.9% uptime SLA, audit log
  export, SSO/SAML, and human-in-the-loop approval policies.

## Dashboard overview

The dashboard has four areas:

1. **Tickets** — create and track support tickets, see workflow progress.
2. **Billing** — invoices, subscription plan, payment methods.
3. **Settings** — team management, feature flags, SSO configuration.
4. **Approvals** — pending human-in-the-loop approval requests (Enterprise).

## Subscriptions and renewal

Subscriptions renew monthly on the anniversary of the start date. The renewal
date is shown in Billing → Subscription. Payment failures are retried three
times over five days before the account is suspended.

## Data retention

Ticket history is retained for the lifetime of the account. Audit logs are
retained for 365 days on Enterprise and 90 days on other plans.

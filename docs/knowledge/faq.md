# Frequently Asked Questions

## Billing

**Why was I charged twice this month?**
Occasionally a payment retry can create a duplicate invoice. If your invoice
shows payment status `duplicate`, the duplicate charge is automatically
eligible for a full refund. Contact support with the invoice id and the refund
will be processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

**How do I change my subscription plan?**
Plan changes are made from the billing section of the dashboard by an account
owner. Upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge; downgrades
take effect at the next renewal date.

**Where can I download my invoices?**
All invoices are available in the dashboard under Billing → Invoice History,
as PDF downloads.

## Account access

**My dashboard is locked. How do I regain access?**
Accounts are locked automatically after repeated failed sign-in attempts or
suspected credential compromise. Support can unlock a locked dashboard after
verifying the account owner. Suspended accounts cannot be unlocked by support;
suspension is a billing state that clears when outstanding invoices are paid.

**How do I add or remove team members?**
Account owners and admins can invite or remove members under Settings → Team.
Removed members lose access immediately.

## Features

**How do I enable a beta feature?**
Beta features are controlled by per-account feature flags. Ask support to
enable a flag for your account, or an admin can request it from Settings →
Features. Flags take effect on the next sign-in.

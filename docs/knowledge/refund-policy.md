# Refund Policy

AgentFlow AI processes refunds for B2B SaaS subscription charges according to
the rules below. These rules are binding for automated agents and human
support staff alike.

## Automatic eligibility

A refund is automatically eligible only when an invoice is a duplicate charge.
An invoice is a duplicate when its payment status is recorded as `duplicate`
in the billing system. Duplicate charges are refunded in full, in the original
currency, to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Charges that are not automatically refundable

- An invoice that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
- An invoice with payment status `pending` has not been paid, so there is
  nothing to refund. The customer should wait for the payment to settle or
  cancel the pending payment with their bank.
- A regular `paid` invoice with no duplicate detected requires manual policy
  review before any refund is issued. Support agents must escalate these cases
  for human approval; they must never promise a refund for a paid invoice.

## Partial refunds and credits

Partial refunds are not issued automatically. When a customer was affected by
a verified SLA breach, service credits are applied to the next invoice instead
of a cash refund, per the Service Level Agreement.

## Enterprise tier

Enterprise-tier customers follow the same eligibility rules. However, refund
requests above 1000 USD always require human approval regardless of payment
status, and the approval must be recorded in the audit log.
